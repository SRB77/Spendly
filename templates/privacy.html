Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Introduction

At Spendly, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our expense tracking application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, and phone number
  • Expense Data: Transaction amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and merchant details
  • Profile Information: Currency preferences, notification settings, and personal preferences
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction patterns (collected via cookies)
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process your expense entries and generate financial insights
  • Authenticate your account and ensure security
  • Send you service-related notifications and updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers with restricted access. We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, regular security audits, and secure authentication protocols to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

All sensitive information, including expense data and personal details, are encrypted both in transit and at rest. We do not store passwords in plain text and use industry-standard hashing algorithms for password storage.

5. Third Party Services

We may use third-party services for specific functions, including:

  • Payment Processing: To securely handle any payments (if applicable)
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our platform
  • Cloud Infrastructure: To host and maintain our servers
  • Email Services: To send notifications and communications

These third parties are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing the services we request.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You can manage your account settings, download your data, or request complete account deletion by visiting your profile settings or contacting our support team.

You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time through your notification preferences, though we may still send service-related notifications necessary for account management.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Spendly uses cookies to enhance your user experience, including session cookies for authentication and analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences, though disabling cookies may limit certain features of the application.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. When you delete your account, all personal data associated with it is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, except where legally required to retain it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes via email or by posting a notice on our website. Your continued use of Spendly following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means you accept and agree to the changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app or mail us at our registered address. We will respond to your inquiry within 15 business days.
